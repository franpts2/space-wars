## About

Space Wars takes you on a nostalgic journey. Test your skills as you dodge enemy fire and
strategize to take down waves of relentless spaceships. Try not to die with your 3 lives.
Push your limits to achieve a new high score in this highly addictive arcade-style game!

This version was developed by **Francisca Portugal**, **João Lopes**, and **Pedro Monteiro**
for LDTS 2024/25 at FEUP.
